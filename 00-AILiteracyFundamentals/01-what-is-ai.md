# What is AI?

</p>

* Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to techniques that allow computers to imitate human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing speech, solving problems, making decisions, and learning from data.
* Machine Learning is a subset of AI in which models learn from data and improve at specific tasks without being explicitly programmed for every situation. It supports technologies such as recommendation systems, spam filters, fraud detection, natural language processing, and image recognition.
* Deep Learning uses multilayered artificial neural networks to identify complex patterns in large datasets. Unlike traditional machine learning, it can learn important features directly from raw data, reducing the need for manual feature engineering.
* Generative AI is a distinct subset of AI focused on producing new content—including text, images, video, and code—in response to prompts. Unlike traditional machine-learning systems that primarily classify information or make predictions, generative AI creates original outputs based on patterns learned from existing data. Examples include ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Midjourney, and DALL-E.
